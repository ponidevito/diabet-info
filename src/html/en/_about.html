Diabetes: Types, Symptoms, and Living with the Condition

Diabetes is a group of chronic metabolic diseases in which the body cannot properly regulate blood glucose (sugar) levels because of insufficient insulin or reduced sensitivity to it. According to the World Health Organization, around 830 million adults worldwide were living with diabetes as of 2022 — roughly one in seven adults on the planet.

Types of Diabetes

The term "diabetes" covers several distinct conditions with different causes — from the autoimmune destruction of pancreatic cells to insulin resistance linked to lifestyle factors.

Type 1 Diabetes
