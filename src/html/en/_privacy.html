Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the DiabetInfo site collects and how it is used.

What data we collect

DiabetInfo is a static informational site. We do not require registration and do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal data through forms on the site. The bread unit calculator runs entirely in your browser — values you enter are never sent anywhere or stored.

Cookies and advertising

The site may use the Google AdSense advertising network and/or web analytics services. These services may set cookies in your browser to display ads (including personalized ads based on your previous visits to sites) and to collect anonymized visit statistics (pages viewed, time on site, approximate location, device type).

Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized Google advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand which pages are read most often. This data is anonymized and used solely to improve the site.

Third-party links

The site contains links to external sources (Wikipedia, the WHO, the IDF, the CDC, the Mayo Clinic, and others). We do not control the privacy practices of these sites — we recommend reviewing them separately before you visit.

Your choices

You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time, or use an ad- or tracker-blocking extension — this will not affect access to the site's informational content.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address.
